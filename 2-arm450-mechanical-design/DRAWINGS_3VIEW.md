# ARM-450 — Orthographic Three-View Drawings

**12 sheets · third-angle projection · hidden detail shown dashed**

```
        TOP
         |
    FRONT --- RIGHT
```

Every sheet carries FRONT, TOP and RIGHT views with overall dimensions, centre lines
through the FRONT view, and a table of **every cylindrical feature the STEP file actually
contains** — diameter, count, axis and depth.

That table is the point. It is generated by reading the B-rep, so a sheet cannot claim a
feature the part does not have. Three sheets carry a red banner because the audit found
their bearing seat missing; see `ARM450_AUDIT.pdf` for the cause and the fix.

**Hidden lines matter here.** The bearing pockets, blind insert holes and internal webs are
precisely the features invisible from outside, and they are what the audit is about. Solid
lines are visible edges; dashed grey lines are edges behind material.

**Views are of the part as modelled**, not as oriented on the print bed. Print orientation
is on the isometric sheets in `ARM450_DRAWINGS_ISO.pdf`.

## 10. Servo horn adapter

![Servo horn adapter](figures/3view/horn_adapter.png)

6 off. **The part that closes the torque path.** Until it existed the servo horn had nothing
to drive: the old solid shaft carried its own horn shoulder, and that disappeared when the
shaft became a bought aluminium tube. The TOP view shows the four Ø1.90 horn holes on the
Ø14 bolt circle and the scalloped face between them; the dashed bore in FRONT is the Ø30.20
slip fit over the tube, and the cross hole is the roll pin.

## Regenerating

```bash
cd ~/ros2_ws/arm450_design
python3 draw_3view.py                    # rewrites figures/3view/*.png
python3 build_pdf.py DRAWINGS_3VIEW.md
```

`orthodraw.py` does the projection and hidden-line removal; `draw_3view.py` lays out the
sheets and pulls the feature table from `audit_parts.py`. Nothing on these sheets is typed
by hand.
